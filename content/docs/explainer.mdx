At the end of this page you have an explainer <Term id="film">film</Term> of 9 s to 3 min. Each <Term id="beat">beat</Term> shows one idea, and a facts list that you checked backs every claim.

You need a source: a link, a paper, a recipe or an outline.

## What this film needs

An explainer teaches. It has no product to sell and no capture to show, so the agent invents the visuals. The picture is the explanation: a diagram that builds, a number that counts, or a process that fills from empty to complete. Type is for labels and one sentence for each beat, never for paragraphs.

The length is 9 s to 3 min (`prompts/story-explainer.md`). Over 60 s, use the director's brief (`prompts/directors-brief-long-form.md`) and one chapter file for each agent. A common length is 30 s in 16:9.

Plan the beat count from the length. The `scene-budget` rule gives 5 to 7 beats of 3 to 6 s for 30 s, and 8 to 12 beats of 4 to 7 s for 60 s. Beat length varies, and the payoff holds longest.

The `hook-payoff` rule puts the question in the first 3 s and the payoff, the beat where the idea becomes clear, at 77 to 90 percent of the length. Do not spend the best fact in the hook.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>

### Write the brief

```bash
bin/vawe new fsync --from prompts/story-explainer.md --length 30 --aspect 16:9
```

The template asks for the source, the audience in one line, the three things the viewer must be able to explain afterwards, the length, the aspect, and the narration. A skipped question takes its default. Read [Write the brief](/docs/brief).

</Step>
<Step>

### Write the facts list and stop

Before any visual, list every claim the film makes, with its source and the line it comes from. Mark anything you could not verify. Copy formulas, dates, numbers and names. Never paraphrase them. The owner checks this list before you draw.

</Step>
<Step>

### Write the beat list and stop

One row for each beat: the time, the one sentence the viewer takes away, what builds on screen, and the cut out. Each beat has one thing that changes. If narration exists, each beat start is a word boundary from the timings, not a guess.

</Step>
<Step>

### Build the page and draft

Draw diagrams as SVG in the page and build them with CSS `@keyframes` or `element.animate()` (a line draws itself with `stroke-dashoffset`). Count numbers with a `[[f, v]]` table and `kf(t, table, ease)` from `core/motion/springs.js`, so the count is a pure function of time.

```bash
bin/vawe dev films/fsync/page.html
```

</Step>
</Steps>

## Narration and captions

Narration is an `<audio src="vo.mp3" data-at="0" data-role="vo">` tag that you supply. `data-role="vo"` ducks the <Term id="bed">bed</Term>. Put music under it at a lower gain. Read [Audio](/docs/audio).

Captions are one `<p>` for each line, timed with the same table as the narration. The `caption-karaoke` and `caption-editorial` moves are built versions. Do not write an em dash on screen.

The agent cannot hear the narration it rendered. Write "pronunciation not checked by the model" in the hand-off, and have a person listen once.

## Pitfalls from the taste digest

- **Show real things working** (`show-real-thing`). The viewer watches the thing work. A value changes and the output updates. A word in a box is not a beat.
- **Text breathing** (`text-breathing`). Readable text fills at most 84 percent of a film. Leave one or two beats with no words.
- **Read lines** (`readable-hold`). A caption of 4 words or more holds 0.6 s a word. A diagram that finishes building in 300 ms was not seen.
- **Every claim is backed** (`claim-backed`). Show each number in the same beat that states it.
- **One thread** (`thread`). Keep one palette and one accent, for the thing explained right now.

A formula with a glyph the font lacks renders a box, so check the glyphs. A count that ends on a rounded number lies: count to the sourced value. Captions and diagram must not share the caption band.

## Common mistakes

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A bullet slide | The beat says the thing instead of showing it | Draw the mechanism |
| A diagram appears at once | No build, or a build of 300 ms | Build it in steps the viewer can follow, then hold it |
| The voice and the picture drift | Beat starts were guessed | Take each start from the timings file |
