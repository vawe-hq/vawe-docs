At the end of this page you have a 1080 x 1920 film for a Reel. Instagram publishes more numbers for Reels ads than for Reels posts, and this page says which is which.

## The limits

The sourced table is in [Video specs by platform](/docs/platforms). Read on 2026-10-31:

- Reels ad (Meta Ads Guide): 9:16, 1440 x 2560, up to 15 minutes, up to 4 GB, MP4 or MOV, stereo AAC at 128 kbps or more, a fixed frame rate.
- Reel post (Instagram): Instagram recommends Reels of 3 minutes or less to people who do not follow you. The feature page says you can record 20 minutes.
- Instagram publishes no loudness target.
- The Instagram help page for Reel sizes did not load text. Its ratio, frame-rate and resolution numbers are not on this page.

## Safe zone

Meta states the safe zone for Reels ads: keep text, logos and key elements out of 14% from the top, 35% from the bottom and 6% from each side. The source is the Meta Ads Guide page for Reels video. For a Reel post, Instagram publishes no pixel values, so treat these numbers as an estimate for posts too.

| Edge | Share | Pixels at 1080 x 1920 |
|---|---|---|
| Top | 14% | 269 |
| Bottom | 35% | 672 |
| Each side | 6% | 65 |

## Make the film

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start a 9:16 page

```bash
bin/vawe new my-reel --request "a 20 second teaser with captions" --aspect 9:16 --length 20
```

The page gets `<meta name="aspect" content="9:16">` and `<meta name="duration" content="20">`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the frame against the safe zone

```bash
bin/vawe still films/my-reel/page.html --at 2 --aspect 9:16
```

Open the PNG in `out/still/`. Text must sit between 269 px and 1248 px from the top (1920 minus 672), and 65 px from each side.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add the music

Add a music file with an `<audio>` tag:

```html
<audio src="assets/music.mp3" loop data-at="0" data-fade-out="0.4"></audio>
```

See [Audio](/docs/audio). To land the cuts on the beat, read [Cut to the beat](/docs/cut-to-the-beat).

</Step>
<Step>

### Draft, then ship

```bash
bin/vawe dev films/my-reel/page.html --aspect 9:16
bin/vawe ship films/my-reel/page.html --aspect 9:16
bin/vawe ship --status --wait
```

The final is H.264 with stereo AAC audio. Check the loudness with `bin/vawe sound films/my-reel/page.html --waveform`.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- `ship` renders 60 fps and has no fps flag. The Meta ad page asks for a fixed frame rate and gives no number. A vawe final has a fixed rate.
- The ad guide lists 1440 x 2560. A vawe 9:16 final is 1080 x 1920, the same ratio at a smaller size. `ship` has no size flag.
- The vawe final audio is AAC at 192 kbps, 48 kHz: above the 128 kbps minimum on the ad page.
