At the end of this page you have a <Term id="film">film</Term> of 6 to 20 s in which type does all the work. It has a hook, a hit, and a last line that still moves.

You need the words. A kinetic film is only as good as its copy, so write the lines first.

## What this film needs

There is no template for this type. Start with `beat-sheet.md`, the template for a short unnarrated film, and take a chain from `prompts/moves/RECIPES.md`:

| Recipe | Length | Thread |
|---|---|---|
| 8 Kinetic type line | 6 s | one dot carries the line: a full stop, then a ball across the cut, then a full stop |
| 13 Kinetic type manifesto | 20 s | one sentence in four beats: the problem, the values, the claim, the name |
| 2 Brand sting, type-led | 5 s | one pulse every 0.48 s (125 bpm); every landing and cut is on it |

Each beat has its own engine, so the type never repeats a trick. In recipe 13 the beats are a soft resolve (`blur-word-cascade`), a slot roll (`word-swap-slot`), a hit (`scale-punch`) and the name (`wordmark-cascade`).

Keep one hit. The <Term id="spectacle">spectacle</Term> is the one word that lands hard, with quiet before it. Do not punch a second word: the same hit twice reads as a template. Land the accent on the claim word, and give it to one word only.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>

### Write the brief

```bash
bin/vawe new one-page-in --from prompts/beat-sheet.md --length 6 --title "One page in."
```

`--title` writes the starter headline into `brief.md` and the page. Fill the Shots and Words tables. The Words table holds each line with its appear and settle seconds, cap height, position and colour.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick the moves by job

The index `prompts/moves/README.md` has a row for each job. Land one hero word: `scale-punch`, `letter-stagger`, `outline-fill`, `variable-weight-wave`. Decide a word on screen: `word-swap-slot`, `flap-resolve`, `text-scramble-decode`. Point at one word: `word-sweep`, `marker-highlight`, `underline-draw`. Change what a word means: `weight-morph`. Read [Motion](/docs/motion) and choose from the clip, not the name.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the key frames, then draft

```bash
bin/vawe frames films/one-page-in/page.html
bin/vawe dev films/one-page-in/page.html
```

Read each line at full size. Then follow the [motion pass](/docs/motion-pass).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Pitfalls from the taste digest

- **Read lines** (`readable-hold`). Cap height is 6 percent of the frame or more. A line holds for max(1.2 s, words / 3). Prose words take 0.6 s each. Add hold, never slow the move.
- **Type scale** (`type-scale`). A frame uses at most six text sizes, and each step is at least 1.25 apart. Pick hero, support and label.
- **Text breathing** (`text-breathing`). A kinetic film is the named exception to the 84 percent limit. Write the exception in the brief.
- **Frame 0 has a subject** (`first-frame`). The first word starts to resolve at 0 s, not after empty ground.
- **Face and ground** (`typeface-default`). Use the kit's face, not Inter or Space Grotesk by habit.
- **Live hold** (`live-hold`). A held line keeps one part alive, such as a caret or the last dot. A camera drift does not count.
- **Exits are faster than entrances** (`exits-shorter`). In recipe 13 an exit runs 0.22 s and an entrance 0.6 s or more.

Three traps from recipe 8 repeat. Launch the ball from the full stop's measured rect and hide the stop on that frame. Read a dot's slot with `offsetLeft` and `offsetTop`, because they ignore transforms. Put no transform on the `h1`: a drifting line reads as a move, not a hold.

## Common mistakes

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Letters land on one frame | The stagger is too small | Stay in the 30 to 80 ms band for each letter |
| The line is hard to read | Cap height under 6 percent | Raise the font size, never shrink the line |
| Two accents compete | Accent on the slot word and the punch word | Give the accent to the claim word only |
