At the end of this page you have a launch <Term id="film">film</Term> of 9 to 40 s that shows a real product doing real things. Every screen in it is a capture of the real site or app, and every colour comes from the site.

You need a vawe checkout, the URL of the product, and the one line the film must prove.

## What this film needs

A launch film has one message. The `brand-launch-from-url` <Term id="template">template</Term> asks for the message in under 12 words and for 3 to 5 moments in the product worth showing. Each moment is one <Term id="beat">beat</Term> with one real capture that proves it.

The length is 9 to 40 s (`prompts/brand-launch-from-url.md`). Under 9 s, use one continuous action instead. A complete launch film of 20 to 30 s follows one of the chains in `prompts/moves/RECIPES.md` (recipes 9 to 14).

The rhythm is a hook, then one move for each beat, then the name. Cuts are not all equal. The `shot-length-varies` rule asks for punchy beats of 0.8 to 1.5 s next to breathing beats of 2 to 3 s, and the payoff holds longest.

The one big moment is the <Term id="spectacle">spectacle</Term>. Put it where `<meta name="spectacle">` says. Leave about 1.5 s of quiet before it: no sound, no travel, nothing arriving.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>

### Build the kit from the site

```bash
node scripts/brand/kit.mjs https://example.com mybrand --init
```

The script writes `assets/brands/mybrand/kit.json` with the site's own palette, fonts and favicon. It also runs the doctor first. Use nothing else for colour and type. Do not improve the palette.

</Step>
<Step>

### Write the brief

```bash
bin/vawe new mybrand-launch --from prompts/brand-launch-from-url.md --length 16 --aspect 16:9
```

The verb asks for the missing details and writes `films/mybrand-launch/page.html` and `brief.md`. Read [Write the brief](/docs/brief) for the answer files and `--defaults`. Fill the Shots, Words and Objects tables before you write the page.

</Step>
<Step>

### Capture the real product

Put each capture in `films/mybrand-launch/assets/`. A real page worked by a cursor is one mp4, not screenshots:

```bash
bin/vawe shot https://example.com --tour steps.json --w 1280 --out films/mybrand-launch/assets/tour.mp4
```

The steps file is a JSON list of `{ t, move | click | scrollTo | scroll, dur }`. Every frame of the tour is made at its exact second.

</Step>
<Step>

### Draft, then read every cut

```bash
bin/vawe dev films/mybrand-launch/page.html
```

Then do the [motion pass](/docs/motion-pass) and the [critique](/docs/critique) in a session that did not write the page. Read [Draft](/docs/draft) for the flags.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Pitfalls from the taste digest

These rules from `taste/build/DIGEST.md` hit a launch film most.

- **Show real things working** (`show-real-thing`). A word in a box is not a beat. A feature you did not see on the site must not appear.
- **Style, not content** (`style-not-content`). Take a reference's grammar and light, never its text, logos or photos.
- **Read lines** (`readable-hold`). Cap height is 6 percent of the frame or more. A line holds for max(1.2 s, words / 3).
- **Exits are faster than entrances** (`exits-shorter`). Exit on `EASE.launch`.
- **No tells** (`no-tells`). No gradient text, glow on UI, bursts or RGB split unless the brief asks.
- **The logo is prominent early and late**, not only on the last beat (`logo-prominence`: at least 5 percent of the frame height).
- **End on motion** (`moving-tail`). The last second keeps one part moving, such as a caret.

A capture scaled below 0.6 loses its type, so crop it instead. A brand accent on a dark surface can fail contrast: the kit says which pair to use.

## Common mistakes

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The film looks like a template | Three cuts in the same direction | Change axis or direction at each cut (recipes list the order) |
| A beat shows a slogan on black | No capture proves the beat | Add the real capture or cut the beat |
| A recipe copied beat for beat | `dev` prints a recipe-echo advice | Replace the `invent` row with a moment of your own |
