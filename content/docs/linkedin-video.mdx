At the end of this page you have a film in a LinkedIn ratio and you know the one ad rule that vawe cannot meet today. LinkedIn video ads ask for less than 30 fps. A vawe final is 60 fps.

## The limits

The sourced table is in [Video specs by platform](/docs/platforms). Read from LinkedIn Help on 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-01.

For a video ad:

- Ratios: 16:9 (640 x 360 to 1920 x 1080), 1:1 (360 x 360 to 1920 x 1920), 4:5 (360 x 450 to 1080 x 1350) and 9:16 (360 x 640 to 1080 x 1920).
- Length: 3 seconds to 30 minutes. LinkedIn suggests 15 to 30 seconds for all placements. In-stream ads run up to 90 seconds.
- File: MP4, H.264 or VP8, 75 KB to 500 MB, frame rate less than 30 fps, AAC or MPEG4 audio.
- Captions: SRT, text only.

For a post:

- Resolution 256 x 144 to 4096 x 2304, aspect 1:2.4 to 2.4:1, 10 to 60 fps, 75 KB to 5 GB.
- Length: 15 minutes on the video-sharing page and 10 minutes on the Page video page.
- LinkedIn publishes no loudness target.

## The 30 fps gap

`bin/vawe ship` renders at 60 fps. Its help lists `--aspect`, `--param`, `--out`, `--wait`, `--status` and `--profile`, and no fps flag. A 60 fps final does not meet the ad rule of less than 30 fps. A 60 fps final is inside the post range of 10 to 60 fps.

For an ad, vawe cannot make the file today. Convert the final to a lower frame rate with your own tool outside vawe. This is a known gap: `ship` needs an fps flag. A draft is 30 fps, which is not less than 30, and it is half size.

## Make the film

<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick the ratio

Use `4:5` for a feed post, `1:1` for a square one and `16:9` for a wide one. `4:5` is the recommended vertical ratio in the ad table.

```bash
bin/vawe new my-post --request "a 20 second product update" --aspect 4:5 --length 20
```

</Step>
<Step>

### Draft

```bash
bin/vawe dev films/my-post/page.html --aspect 4:5
```

See [Draft](/docs/draft). A 4:5 final is 1080 x 1350, which is the maximum on the ad table.

</Step>
<Step>

### Ship

```bash
bin/vawe ship films/my-post/page.html --aspect 4:5
bin/vawe ship --status --wait
```

To make a 1:1 and a 16:9 from the same page, run `bin/vawe ship films/my-post/page.html --aspect all`. It writes five files, one per common aspect.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

- Ads: the 60 fps final fails the frame-rate rule. See above.
- Captions: LinkedIn takes captions as an SRT file. Captions in a vawe film are part of the picture, and vawe writes no SRT.
- Loudness: LinkedIn publishes no target. See [Loudness and the mix](/docs/loudness-and-the-mix).
