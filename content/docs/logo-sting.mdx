At the end of this page you have a <Term id="film">film</Term> of 3 to 8 s in which motion is the message: a logo reveal, a stat hit or a moving title. A sting has no narration.

You need a name for the brand. If the brand does not exist, invent it. The example film `films/examples/colour-sting` does this.

## What this film needs

A request is a sting when it is under 10 s and motion carries the message (`guides/ROUTING.md`, row 9). A 6 s logo reveal for a real brand is still a sting. A film "from this site" with no length is a [launch video](/docs/launch-video).

A sting has one <Term id="beat">beat</Term> or a few. Under 15 s most films are one continuous action, so plan the thread first: one object, type line, colour or rhythm that stays through every cut. The `thread` rule asks for it.

Put the <Term id="spectacle">spectacle</Term> in the middle. In `colour-sting` the page sets `<meta name="spectacle" content="2.75">` in a 5 s film: the ring lands as the "o" of the name. Quiet comes before it.

Two recipes in `prompts/moves/RECIPES.md` are 5 s stings:

| Recipe | Led by | Thread |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Brand sting | graphic or colour | the name: a window, then a rider on the colour block, then the lockup word |
| 2 Brand sting | type | a rhythm: one pulse every 0.48 s (125 bpm); every landing and cut is on it |

Each recipe has an `invent` row. Do not copy it. Design that beat from the brand's own mark, colour or name.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>

### Write the brief with the beat sheet

```bash
bin/vawe new onsen --from prompts/beat-sheet.md --length 5 --aspect 16:9
```

`beat-sheet.md` is the template for a sting: one table with one row for each shot. When no template fits, `new` takes a sting by default (`bin/vawe new --help`). Fill the Shots, Words and Objects tables in `brief.md` before the page. Each Shots row has one focal action. The slowest row is at least 3 times the fastest.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the first frame

```bash
bin/vawe frames films/onsen/page.html
```

The `first-frame` rule says the subject shows by 0.1 s, and the ground is never empty for the first 0.5 s. Read the stills at full size.

</Step>
<Step>

### Draft and read the cuts

```bash
bin/vawe dev films/onsen/page.html
bin/vawe strip films/onsen/page.html --cuts
```

Read [Draft](/docs/draft) and [Motion pass](/docs/motion-pass). In a 5 s film each seam matters, so look at every one.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add sound last, and keep it small

A sting takes a few <Term id="cue">cues</Term>, placed 30 ms before their frame. Do not add a synth <Term id="bed">bed</Term>. Read [Audio](/docs/audio).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Pitfalls from the taste digest

- **End on motion** (`moving-tail`). A wordmark may land and hold, but one part of the lockup moves in the last second. A camera push does not count.
- **Use the sheen band once** (`no-tells`). The `logo-sting` move and the `mark-trace` move each carry a stock device. Take one of them, not both.
- **The logo is large enough** (`logo-prominence`): at least 5 percent of the frame height, about 7 percent in practice.
- **Choose the hero from the direction**, not from the attractor list (`attractors`): a glowing orb, a dusk sun, a lens streak, an accent bar. A disc needs a light source and a surface.

Two traps from recipe 1 cost time. Swap shots under the colour block, never before it. Start `logo-sting` when the trailing block passes the centre, or the mark lands under the block.

## Common mistakes

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The mark lands under another layer | The move starts before the block clears | Start the move when the block passes the centre |
| The tail freezes | The push runs a fixed time | Set the push to the time left |
| The sting reads as a template | A recipe followed 1:1 | Change at least two moves and design the `invent` beat |
