At the end of this page you have a <Term id="film">film</Term> of 12 to 60 s in which a cursor uses an interface and the interface answers. Every click has a visible result.

You need a product. For a real product you need its URL. For an invented product the brief must say to invent the interface.

## What this film needs

A UI demo shows a real artifact working. The `show-real-thing` rule is the first test: a click with no result is not a beat, and chrome with no function is not a beat. A real product is shown as it is, from captures or from its own DOM. An invented product gets a real interface, designed for the film.

Pick the template from the subject:

| Subject | Template | Length |
|---|---|---|
| a real product or site | `brand-launch-from-url.md` | 9 to 40 s |
| an invented product | `beat-sheet.md` | the rows you plan |
| a mechanism you can drive | `interactive-lab-capture.md` | 20 to 60 s |
| one element that becomes many UI states | `ui-morph-loop.md` (see [Loops](/docs/loops)) | 12 to 16 s |

Three chains in `prompts/moves/RECIPES.md` fit a UI demo. Recipe 10 is an AI product demo of 22 s: a prompt becomes an answer, the answer becomes a deliverable, and the deliverable is sent. Recipe 11 is a feature tour of 20 s on one canvas, with a camera that pans between stations. Recipe 9 is a problem-to-fix launch of 21 s.

The <Term id="spectacle">spectacle</Term> is usually the result: the check that lands, the figure that settles, the status the camera zooms into. Quiet comes before it, with the interface held still for about 2 s.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>

### Write the brief

```bash
bin/vawe new ledger-demo --from prompts/brand-launch-from-url.md --length 22 --aspect 16:9
```

Swap the template for the subject. Fill the Shots, Words and Objects tables. List each capture with its source URL.

</Step>
<Step>

### Capture a real flow as one clip

```bash
bin/vawe shot https://example.com --tour steps.json --w 1280 --out films/ledger-demo/assets/tour.mp4
```

A real page worked by a cursor is one mp4, not screenshots. Hover and click are real events. Add `--scale 3` for a close-up that must stay sharp.

</Step>
<Step>

### Mark the UI so the checks read it

Put UI that is the subject inside an element with `data-ui`. Its text floor is 3 percent of the frame height. Product labels that only add texture take `data-chrome`, with a floor of 2.5 percent. Other copy needs a cap height of 6 percent. Read [Text and fonts](/docs/text-and-fonts).

</Step>
<Step>

### Draft and read the click frames

```bash
bin/vawe dev films/ledger-demo/page.html
bin/vawe strip films/ledger-demo/page.html --cuts
```

Check that the frame after each click shows what the click did. Then do the [motion pass](/docs/motion-pass).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Moves that fit

The index in `prompts/moves/README.md` lists them by job. Show a click doing its job: `cursor-click`, `notification-pop`, `success-check`, `command-palette-summon`. Show a real UI arriving: `card-assemble`, `skeleton-reveal`, `clip-expand`, `device-tilt-stage`. Point at one part: `spotlight-dim`, `bracket-callout`, `ui-focus-zoom`. Show an AI product answering: `ai-stream-response`. Show an agent doing steps: `agent-progress`, `terminal-run`. Read [Motion](/docs/motion).

## Pitfalls from the taste digest

- **Feedback follows its cause** (`cause-first`). A burst comes after the press. A sound lands on its action.
- **The camera holds still** (`constant-camera`). One move at the spectacle at most. A part moving inside the frame keeps a hold alive.
- **Read lines** (`readable-hold`). A UI screen dwells at least 1.2 s (`shot-length-varies`).
- **No tells** (`no-tells`). No glow on UI chrome unless the brief asks.

Four traps come from the recipes. Use one window for `clip-expand` and `ai-stream-response`, with the same pixels at the swap. A capture cannot morph, so rebuild only the button as live DOM when a move must animate it. Exit the cursor down before each pan, or it streaks with the pan. Pick a zoom target with no glyph under it, such as a status pill.

## Common mistakes

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The click shows no result | The next frame is not the done state | Add the result frame, or cut the click |
| UI text is hard to read | The mock is scaled below its floor | Crop the capture instead of scaling it |
| Two cursor clicks look the same | A template pattern | Change the move, as recipe 11 does with `card-assemble` |
