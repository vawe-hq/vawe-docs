At the end of this page you have a 1080 x 1920 film of 180 seconds or less that YouTube files as a Short. The same page can also make the 16:9 film: see [Vertical and square films](/docs/vertical-social) for the layout and [YouTube](/docs/youtube) for the wide upload.

## The limits

The sourced table is in [Video specs by platform](/docs/platforms). Read from YouTube Help on 2026-10-31:

- A video with a square or vertical aspect ratio, up to three minutes long, is a Short. YouTube states this for uploads from 2024-10-15.
- The maximum Shorts resolution is 1080p. YouTube publishes no pixel size, no frame rate and no file size for Shorts.
- YouTube states no loudness target.

The vawe 9:16 final is 1080 x 1920, which is inside the 1080p limit.

## Safe zone (estimate)

YouTube publishes no pixel values for the Shorts interface.

As an estimate, use the only official numbers for a vertical frame that this guide found: the Meta ad guide for Reels asks for 14% from the top, 35% from the bottom and 6% from each side. These numbers are for Instagram ads. They are not a YouTube rule. On a 1080 x 1920 frame they are:

| Edge | Share | Pixels |
|---|---|---|
| Top | 14% | 269 |
| Bottom | 35% | 672 |
| Each side | 6% | 65 |

Treat the box that remains as the place for text. Use `bin/vawe still` to look at the frame (see below).

## Make the film

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start a 9:16 page under 3 minutes

```bash
bin/vawe new my-short --request "a 30 second tip about the product" --aspect 9:16 --length 30
```

The verb writes `<meta name="aspect" content="9:16">` and `<meta name="duration" content="30">` into the page. Keep the duration at 180 or less.

</Step>
<Step>

### Look at a vertical frame

```bash
bin/vawe still films/my-short/page.html --at 3 --aspect 9:16
```

Open the PNG in `out/still/` and check that the text sits inside the box above. Read [Aspect ratios](/docs/aspect-ratios): the page lays out with `--vw` and `--vh`, never with a crop.

</Step>
<Step>

### Draft, then ship

```bash
bin/vawe dev films/my-short/page.html --aspect 9:16
bin/vawe ship films/my-short/page.html --aspect 9:16
bin/vawe ship --status --wait
```

`ship` needs a fresh critique and the owner approval. See [Ship](/docs/ship).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Sound and captions

The moves `caption-karaoke` and `caption-editorial` in `prompts/moves/` are two caption styles for words on screen. YouTube gives no loudness target, so the default mix is a safe start: it lands near -20 LUFS. See [Loudness and the mix](/docs/loudness-and-the-mix).

## Limits

- A final is 60 fps. `ship` has no fps flag, and YouTube publishes no frame rate for Shorts.
- The Shorts upload help page says "vertical". The newer three-minute page says "square or vertical". This guide follows the newer page.
- A film of exactly 180 seconds is at the limit. Leave a margin.
