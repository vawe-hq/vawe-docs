At the end of this page you have a 1920 x 1080 mp4 that meets the YouTube encoding page. For a vertical film under 3 minutes, read [YouTube Shorts](/docs/youtube-shorts).

## The limits

The full table with sources is in [Video specs by platform](/docs/platforms). The rows that matter here, read from the YouTube Help pages on 2026-10-31:

- 16:9 is the standard aspect on a computer. YouTube also accepts vertical and square uploads.
- A file is 256 GB or 12 hours, whichever is less. An unverified account uploads 15 minutes at most by default.
- YouTube asks for H.264, an MP4 container with the moov atom at the front, AAC-LC audio at 48 kHz, and BT.709 colour for SDR.
- At 1080p, YouTube lists 8 Mbps for 24 to 30 fps and 12 Mbps for 48 to 60 fps.
- YouTube does not state a loudness target on these pages.

## Make the film

<Steps>
<Step>

### Start a 16:9 page

```bash
bin/vawe new my-video --request "a 45 second product intro" --aspect 16:9 --length 45
```

The verb writes `films/my-video/page.html` with `<meta name="aspect" content="16:9">` and `<meta name="duration" content="45">`. Change the duration in the page if the film grows.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the draft

```bash
bin/vawe dev films/my-video/page.html
```

A draft is half size at 30 fps with the sound mixed. See [Draft](/docs/draft).

</Step>
<Step>

### Measure the sound

```bash
bin/vawe sound films/my-video/page.html --waveform
```

The verb prints the LUFS and the true peak of the mix. Without a `<meta name="loudness">`, the level is what the cues add up to. See [Loudness and the mix](/docs/loudness-and-the-mix).

</Step>
<Step>

### Ship the final

```bash
bin/vawe ship films/my-video/page.html --aspect 16:9
bin/vawe ship --status --wait
```

`ship` needs a fresh critique and the owner approval first. See [Ship](/docs/ship). The master is `out/my-video.mp4`.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What the final contains

The final is 60 fps, H.264 High profile, `yuv420p`, with `+faststart` (the moov atom at the front). The audio is AAC at 48 kHz. These match the YouTube page for codec, container, sample rate and fast start. Upload `out/my-video.mp4`. Do not upload the `.web.mp4` copy beside it.

YouTube asks to upload at the recording frame rate. A vawe final is always 60 fps, because `ship` has no fps flag. The 60 fps row is on the YouTube list (48 to 60 fps).

## Limits

- Colour: the encode arguments in `harness/media/render-page.mjs` set no colour tag, and no code in `harness/media/` names BT.709. The YouTube page recommends BT.709 for SDR. Check the colour of a final with your own tools before a colour-critical upload.
- B-frames and GOP: the YouTube page lists 2 B-frames and a closed GOP. vawe passes only `-profile:v high -preset fast -crf 16 -x264-params aq-mode=3`, so these settings are the x264 defaults.
- Bitrate: `-crf 16` is a quality target, so `ship` sets no bitrate.
